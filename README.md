# Myntra-Sales-Dashboard

## Introduction

The **Myntra Sales Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product trends using interactive visualizations. The dashboard provides meaningful business insights that help understand sales growth, category performance, and customer preferences.



## Aim

* Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.
* Identify top-performing product categories and brands.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Visualize key business metrics using Power BI.
* Generate actionable insights for business decision-making.



## Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence Analytics
* Sales Data Analysis



## Role of DAX in the Project

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) was used extensively in this dashboard to perform calculations, aggregations, and business analysis on the Myntra sales dataset.

### Key DAX Calculations Used

* **SUM of Sales** → Used to calculate total revenue generated by each brand.
* **SUM of Ratings** → Helped analyze overall customer satisfaction across brands.
* **SUM of Number of Ratings** → Used to identify highly engaged and popular products.
* **SUM of Price & MRP** → Enabled comparison between selling price and original market price.
* **Discount Percentage Analysis** → Used to measure how discounts influenced customer purchasing behavior.
* **Calculated Measures for KPIs** → Created dynamic KPI cards for revenue, ratings, and product performance.
* **Brand-wise Aggregations** → Helped compare performance between multiple brands using grouped calculations.

### Why DAX Was Important

* It transformed raw e-commerce data into meaningful business metrics.
* Enabled dynamic calculations that automatically updated with filters and slicers.
* Improved decision-making by providing accurate performance comparisons.
* Helped uncover trends related to pricing, discounts, customer ratings, and sales performance.
* Enhanced dashboard interactivity and analytical depth beyond basic visualizations.

The use of DAX played a major role in making the dashboard more interactive, insightful, and business-focused.

* Certain product categories generated significantly higher sales compared to others.
